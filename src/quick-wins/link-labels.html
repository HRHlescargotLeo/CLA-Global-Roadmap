Quick win 2 of 24 · Clear "View details" links

What we're proposing

Give every "View details" link on the Locations lists a label that names the firm, so people using screen readers can tell the links apart.

Why

The hidden text that screen readers announce for each "View details" link reads ": City/State", which looks like a placeholder left in the template. There are 57 of these links across the two lists, and they all sound the same. The fix is one line in the template, and it is the sort of thing an accessibility audit picks up straight away.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
