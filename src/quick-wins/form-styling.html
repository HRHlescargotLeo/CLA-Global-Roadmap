Quick win 23 of 24 · HubSpot forms in the site's style

What we're proposing

Style the HubSpot forms with the site's own type, field and button styles, so they look like part of the page.

Why

The forms use HubSpot's default look: small labels, pale grey fields and HubSpot's own button, inside a site that is otherwise navy, Georgia and light sans. It is a theme setting in HubSpot plus a little CSS, and makes the most important step on the site look as considered as the rest of it.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
