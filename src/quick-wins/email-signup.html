Quick win 11 of 24 · Email sign-up

What we're proposing

An email sign-up in the site footer and on the Insights page, with a choice of topics and regions, feeding a HubSpot list.

Why

We couldn't find a way to sign up for updates anywhere on the site: not on the homepage, the insights pages or in the footer. Member firms publish useful, timely pieces, such as Malta's move to digital VAT, but the only way to hear about them is to come back and look. HubSpot is already in place for the forms, so the list has somewhere to live.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
