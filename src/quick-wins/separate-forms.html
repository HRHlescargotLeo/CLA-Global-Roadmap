Quick win 15 of 24 · Separate forms for each audience

What we're proposing

Give clients, firms that want to join and the press their own short forms on the pages they belong to, instead of one form that changes shape.

Why

One HubSpot form serves everyone. Choosing "I want to join" from "How can we help?" expands it from ten fields to 21, adding turnover, currency, public interest entity clients and three revenue percentages, all required. Separate forms would be shorter for everyone, and each one can be tracked as its own conversion in HubSpot and GA4.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
