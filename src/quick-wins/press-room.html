Quick win 12 of 24 · A press room

What we're proposing

Turn the Media page into a press room: a named media contact, the network boilerplate with a copy button, key facts and brand assets, above the news list.

Why

Media is a list of news items, mostly announcements of new members. There is no press contact on the page, no boilerplate a journalist can paste, and no logos to download. The "media enquiry" option on the contact form is the only route in, which is slow for anyone on a deadline.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
