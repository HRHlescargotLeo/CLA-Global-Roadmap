Quick win 20 of 24 · One spelling standard

What we're proposing

Choose one spelling standard for the site, set it out in a one-page style guide, and correct the main pages.

Why

Pages mix US and UK spelling, sometimes within a few lines: "realize", "organization" and "digitized" sit alongside "organise", "organisations" and "specialises". Either standard is fine. Mixing them reads as if no one has checked, which isn't true and isn't the impression the network wants to give. UK spelling would suit a network registered in London, but that is CLA Global's call.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
