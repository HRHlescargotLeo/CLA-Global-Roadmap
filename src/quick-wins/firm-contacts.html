Quick win 1 of 24 · Contacts on the firm list

What we're proposing

Pull each firm's main contact, city and email from its profile onto the Locations list, with a button to get in touch. Nothing else about the list changes.

Why

Every card on the network and alliance lists has address, email and phone fields in its markup, but they are all empty. To find a person you have to open each profile in turn, which on a phone means a lot of back and forth. The profiles already hold the contacts, so this is a template change rather than new content.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
