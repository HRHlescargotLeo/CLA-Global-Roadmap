Quick win 14 of 24 · Say what happens next

What we're proposing

After someone sends an enquiry, tell them who will reply, by when, and that a copy is on its way to their inbox.

Why

Nothing on the contact page says who reads an enquiry or how long a reply takes. A prospective client sending their first message to an international network is taking a small leap, and "we'll reply within two working days" costs nothing to say, provided the network can keep to it.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
