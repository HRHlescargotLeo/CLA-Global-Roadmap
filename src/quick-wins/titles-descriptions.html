Quick win 21 of 24 · Page titles and descriptions

What we're proposing

Set a title and description pattern for each page type, and fill in the pages that are missing them, so search results and shared links read properly.

Why

Join, Leadership, Why CLA Global? and Locations have no meta description, so search engines make one up from whatever text they find. Leadership, Why CLA Global? and Our Brand and Values have no "| CLA Global" in their titles, and the search results page has no title at all.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
