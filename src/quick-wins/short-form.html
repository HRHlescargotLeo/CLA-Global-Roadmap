Quick win 13 of 24 · A shorter client form

What we're proposing

Cut the client enquiry form from ten fields to six: phone becomes optional, country becomes a list, and "How did you find out about us?" moves to the confirmation page.

Why

For a client, the current form is ten fields, eight of them required, including phone number and "How did you find out about us?". Country is free text, while the phone field next to it has a list of 235 countries. Every required field is a reason for someone to give up, and none of these is needed to reply to an enquiry.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
