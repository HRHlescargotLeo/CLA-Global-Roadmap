Quick win 16 of 24 · Headline numbers in the page

What we're proposing

Write the headline figures into the page itself, so search engines and link previews show 15,000+, 235+ and 1,600+ instead of zeros.

Why

The figures count up from zero as you scroll, which looks fine in a browser. But the page HTML says "0+" for all three, and that is what search engines, link previews and anyone with JavaScript switched off see. The same block appears on four pages. Keeping the count-up as an extra while the real numbers sit in the HTML fixes it without changing the look.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
