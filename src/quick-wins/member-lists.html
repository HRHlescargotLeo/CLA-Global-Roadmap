Quick win 19 of 24 · Member lists kept current

What we're proposing

Check the network and alliance lists against membership records, and add a short note to old news about firms that have since left, with a link to current firms in that country.

Why

Search results and news still announce some firms joining that don't appear on today's lists. For example, a 2024 news item welcomes an alliance member in a country that is no longer listed. Firms come and go in any network, but a reader landing on that page from a search has no way of knowing the firm has moved on.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
