What we're proposing

A short timeline of how the network has grown since July 2022, drawn from the news the site already carries.

Why

The network's growth is one of its best arguments for joining, but on the site it is told only through "welcomes new member" headlines spread across seven pages of Media. Pulled together, the same facts make a much stronger case: founded in 2022, in the top 25 networks by early 2023, a member of the Forum of Firms this year, four new countries in a few months.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
