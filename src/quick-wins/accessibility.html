Quick win 22 of 24 · Accessibility fixes

What we're proposing

Fix the accessibility issues found in the review: label the search field, add alt text to meaningful images, and give links proper labels. Then check the main templates against WCAG 2.2 AA.

Why

On the homepage, ten of the 19 images have no alt text (some are decorative, which is fine, but not all), the search box that opens from the header has no label, and the firm lists carry placeholder link text. None of these is hard to fix, and together they make a noticeable difference to anyone using a screen reader.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
