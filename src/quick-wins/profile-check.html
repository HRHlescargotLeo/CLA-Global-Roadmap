Quick win 3 of 24 · Firm contacts kept current

What we're proposing

A short check that each member firm completes every six months, confirming or updating the contacts on its profile. The global office sees which firms are up to date.

Why

Firm profiles are one of the most useful things on the site, and also the easiest to let slip. On the CLA Malta profile, for example, two of the four partner emails use a different domain from the firm's own website, which suggests a change that never reached the profile. Asking firms to confirm their entry twice a year is a small habit that keeps the directory trustworthy.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
