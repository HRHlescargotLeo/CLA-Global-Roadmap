Quick win 6 of 24 · Network or alliance, side by side

What we're proposing

A side-by-side comparison of network and alliance membership, with a two-question check that suggests which route suits a firm.

Why

The Locations page describes alliance membership as giving firms the benefits of CLA Global "without the requirements of a network membership", but those requirements are never listed anywhere. A managing partner reading that has to enquire to find out what they'd be signing up to. A table, even one that leaves fees as a basis rather than a figure, answers the question that comes before every membership enquiry.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
