Quick win 4 of 24 · Named specialists on service pages

What we're proposing

End each service page with three named specialists from member firms, each with a contact button, instead of a single generic Contact Us.

Why

Of the four service pages, only Advisory names anyone. Audit, Tax and Outsourcing each finish with a Contact Us button that leads to the general form. A finance director deciding whether a network can handle their transfer pricing wants to see who would actually do the work, and three names says more than a paragraph about global teams.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
