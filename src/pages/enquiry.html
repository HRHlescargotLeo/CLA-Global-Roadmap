Prototype 3 of 5 · Cross-border enquiry and RFP

What we're proposing

An enquiry that starts with where the client needs help and what with, then shows the member firms and named contacts who cover it before asking for any personal details. The client can contact one firm directly or let CLA Global coordinate. The same flow handles a request for proposal, with a timeline, a deadline and a document upload, and it ends by saying who will reply and when.

Why

Clients, journalists and firms that want to join all share one HubSpot form today. For a client it is ten fields, eight of them required, including phone number and "How did you find out about us?", and nothing on the page says who replies or when. It asks for the visitor's own country but not the countries they need help in, so every enquiry has to be read and routed by hand in the global office. Showing the right contacts first will cut that, and some clients will simply email the partner, which is arguably the better outcome anyway.

Try it from a firm profile ("Ask this firm") to see the firm carried through, or from here with two or three countries.
