What we're proposing

The detailed questions from today's join form, moved into a questionnaire that a firm is sent after its first call. It is split into short sections, uses bands and tick-boxes instead of free text, checks that the revenue split adds up to 100%, and can be saved and finished later.

Why

The information is reasonable to ask for. The timing is the problem. Today it is 21 required free-text fields on a public contact form, answered before anyone has spoken to the firm, and the answers arrive in formats nobody can compare ("about 40", "40-50", "forty"). Asking later, in bands, gets cleaner answers from firms that are already interested.

Questions follow the current claglobal.com join form. Band ranges are placeholders for CLA Global to set.
