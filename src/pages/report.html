What we're proposing

An annual survey of privately owned, mid-market businesses with cross-border plans, published as a web report: headline findings, charts that can be cut by region, chapters and a PDF. It gives the LinkedIn campaign, member firms and the press something new to talk about every year.

Why

We found no research programme on claglobal.com. All five networks we compared run one, and most are into their second or later edition. CLA Global's positioning names its audience more precisely than most of them do, which is the hard part of designing a survey. This is the largest idea in the pack, and the web page is the smaller part of the work: the survey itself would need a research partner and a budget.

Title, questions and every figure on this page are invented to show the format. Nothing here is real data.
