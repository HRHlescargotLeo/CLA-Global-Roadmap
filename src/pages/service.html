What we're proposing

Each service page lists its specialisms as real sections with their own headings, names three people who lead the service in member firms, says how many firms offer it and where, and shows the latest related insights. Every specialism links to the firms that offer it.

Why

The Tax page mentions transfer pricing, global mobility and indirect tax, but as bullet points and bold text inside one long page. Audit, Tax and Outsourcing each have two headings in total: the page title and "Contact Us". Only Advisory names anyone. A finance director comparing networks wants to know who does the work and in which countries, and at the moment the page can't tell them.

Tax is written out in full. The other three services use the same template with placeholder copy: try the service links under the title.
