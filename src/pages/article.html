What we're proposing

Articles that lead somewhere. The author gets a card with their firm and country and a button to talk to that firm, tags link back into the filtered hub, related pieces follow, and the long legal disclaimer folds away.

Why

Today an article links its authors only to their LinkedIn profiles and ends with a long disclaimer. A reader who has just learned that Malta is introducing domestic e-invoicing has no route to the firm that wrote about it, which is a shame, because that reader is about as qualified a lead as the site will get.
