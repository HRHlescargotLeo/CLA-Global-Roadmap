Prototype 5 of 5 · Insights and research hub

What we're proposing

An insights hub that can be searched and filtered by type, service, industry and country, with case studies and research alongside articles. A flagship annual report gets its own page, articles link to the author's firm, and readers can sign up for email updates on the topics they care about.

Why

Member firms publish useful, local pieces (Malta and ViDA, UAE and US tax residency, Pillar Two), but the Insights page is six pages with no filter or search, and the same two "featured" items sit at the top of every page. There is no email sign-up anywhere on the site. There is also no research programme, which is the one thing every comparable network we looked at has. For a network positioned on mid-market businesses with cross-border ambitions, an annual survey of exactly those businesses would be hard to beat as a reason to visit.

Titles and dates of articles are from claglobal.com. Service, industry and country tags, case studies and the report are sample content.
