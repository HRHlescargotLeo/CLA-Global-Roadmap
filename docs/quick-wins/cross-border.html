Quick win 5 of 24 · How we work across borders

What we're proposing

A short page explaining how member firms work together for a client with interests in several countries: one lead contact, joined-up teams, shared standards, and a worked example.

Why

"Cross-border needs" appears in the positioning on almost every page, but nothing on the site explains what working across borders with CLA Global looks like in practice. Who is the client's contact? How do firms in different countries share the work? A single page answering those questions would give every service page and the homepage something concrete to link to.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.

ProposedNew page, linked from service pages and the homepage

How we work across borders

One lead contact. Teams in each country who know the local rules. One plan.

  1. One lead contact

    Your adviser in your home country coordinates everything.

  2. Local teams

    Member firms in each country handle the local work.

  3. Shared standards

    Aligned quality and risk policies across the network.

  4. One timetable

    Deadlines and reporting agreed across all countries.

Example: a UK retailer with subsidiaries in Spain and Ireland (sample)

S&W in London led the group audit. CLA Spain and S&W Ireland audited the local subsidiaries to the same plan and deadline, and the client dealt with one partner throughout.

ContentThe worked example is invented to show the format. A real one would need a client's permission.
