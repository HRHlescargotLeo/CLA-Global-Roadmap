How to review

  • Each quick win stands alone. Where it helps, "Today" shows the current site, rebuilt from what we saw, beside the "Proposed" version. Use the Both / Today / Proposed switch to look at one at a time.
  • Switch Notes on (top right) on any quick win to see what it proposes and why.
  • Use the numbered bar at the top, or the previous and next links at the foot of each page, to move through them in order.
