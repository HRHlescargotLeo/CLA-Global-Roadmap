Quick win 18 of 24 · Leadership with biographies

What we're proposing

Add a photo and a short biography for each of the board members and global office contacts, with board members linked to their firm's profile.

Why

The Leadership page lists 13 people with their name, role, firm and a LinkedIn link, and nothing more. For a firm weighing up membership, the people running the network are part of the decision, and a few lines on each person's background would go a long way.

Can be done on its own, without any of the big improvements.
