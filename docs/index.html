Five ideas for claglobal.com

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the CLA Global website. They are deliberately greyscale, so the conversation stays on structure and behaviour; the CLA Global look comes in the next round.

Version 1 30 September 2026 Greyscale prototypes Sample data

The prototypes

  1. Find a firm
    1

    Find a CLA Global firm

    One directory for all 57 network and alliance firms, searchable and filterable, with a map and a named contact on every card. Each firm gets a profile page with a country guide.

    Try: filter to Tax in Europe, switch to the map, then open a firm's profile and its "Doing business in" tab.

    Open prototype
  2. For clients
    2

    A front door for clients

    A homepage with separate routes for clients and for firms, plus a service page with named specialists and an industry page built from the firm profiles.

    Try: search for Dubai with Tax from the homepage, then come back and open Manufacturing and distribution.

    Open prototype
  3. Enquiry and RFP
    3

    Cross-border enquiry and proposal request

    Countries and services first, then the firms and people who cover them, then a short form. Clients can ask a question or request a proposal, and the confirmation says who will reply.

    Try: add Mexico and the United States, choose Tax, pick "Request a proposal" and follow it through.

    Open prototype
  4. Join CLA Global
    4

    Join CLA Global

    A join page that makes the case, compares network and alliance membership and ends in a seven-field expression of interest. The detailed questions move to a questionnaire sent after a first call.

    Try: register interest at the foot of the page, then open the questionnaire and move the revenue split sliders.

    Open prototype
  5. Insights
    5

    Insights and research hub

    Insights filtered by type, service, industry and country, an email sign-up, articles that link to the author's firm, and a flagship research report.

    Try: filter to Case studies, then Tax, then sign up for email updates.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: search, filters, the map, tabs, the enquiry steps, forms and their validation. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for CLA Global (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Firm names, countries and cities come from claglobal.com. People, emails, figures, services, industries and case studies are sample data. Emails all end @example.com.
  • Greyscale is deliberate. Please comment on what is there and how it works; colour, type and photography come next.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
