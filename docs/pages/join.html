Prototype 4 of 5 · Join CLA Global

What we're proposing

One page that takes a firm from first interest to a short expression of interest: the benefits in brief, network and alliance membership side by side, what the network looks for, the process with rough timings, member stories, how fast the network has grown and the questions firms usually ask. The detailed questionnaire moves to a second stage, sent after a first call.

Why

The current Join page is a heading, a HubSpot form and a line that begins "If you are unable to see the form below". The benefits and member quotes are on a different page. Choosing "I want to join" on the form expands it to 21 required fields, all free text, including turnover, currency, number of public interest entity clients and three revenue percentages. That is a lot to ask of a managing partner who is only curious, and alliance membership is described as coming "without the requirements of a network membership" without ever saying what those requirements are.

Benefits, quotes and growth milestones are from claglobal.com. Process timings, events and FAQ answers are placeholders for CLA Global to confirm.
