Prototype 1 of 5 · Firm profile

What we're proposing

One profile template for every firm, network or alliance. Headline figures and named contacts at the top, services and industries as links back into the finder, a short guide to doing business in that country, and the firm's own insights. A single "Ask this firm" button starts an enquiry with the firm already chosen.

Why

The current profiles have the right ingredients but present them unevenly. Industry lists run together with full stops and no spaces, the UK page is titled "CLA United Kingdom" although the firm is S&W, and two of the four partner emails on the Malta profile use a different domain from the firm's website. A template with structured fields fixes the presentation once, and gives each firm a reason to keep its entry up to date.

Open any firm from the finder, or change the address to firm.html?firm=dhpg. People, emails and figures are sample data.
