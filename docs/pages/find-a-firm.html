Prototype 1 of 5 · Find a CLA Global firm

What we're proposing

One directory for every network and alliance firm, searchable by country, city or name and filtered by region, service and industry, with a map. Each result shows who to contact, and every firm gets a profile page with its people, services, industries, a short guide to doing business in that country and its latest insights.

Why

Today the firms sit on two separate lists, 23 network entries on one page and 34 alliance entries on another, with no search, filter or map. On a phone the network list alone runs to about seven screens. The address, email and phone fields on every card are empty, so the only way to find a person is to open each profile in turn. The profiles themselves hold good data (contacts, services, industries, headcount), so most of what this needs already exists in the CMS. It just isn't being put to work.

Firm names, countries and cities are from claglobal.com. Contacts, services, industries and figures are sample data.

BehaviourResults update as you type. Country names autocomplete. The search also matches city and contact names, so "Dubai" or a partner's surname both work.

Membership
Network or alliance?

Network firms carry the CLA Global name and brand. Alliance firms are independent firms with access to the network's reach, under their own name. Both work with each other on client matters.

BehaviourNetwork and alliance firms appear in one list, labelled. Filters combine, and the current search is kept in the page address so it can be shared.
