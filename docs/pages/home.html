Prototype 2 of 5 · A front door for clients

What we're proposing

A homepage that serves both audiences from the first screen: a firm search for businesses that need help abroad, and a clear route for firms thinking of joining. Below that, the four services, industry pages, client case studies and the latest insights, with the headline numbers written into the page. The prototype also includes a service page and an industry page built the same way.

Why

The current homepage is, in effect, a recruitment page. "Why Join CLA Global?" appears twice, followed by member benefits, a join video and "Become a Member". A mid-market business looking for an adviser in another country gets four insight cards and nothing else, and those show March to July, oldest first. The positioning is all about clients with cross-border needs, so the homepage ought to have something for them. The recruitment case doesn't disappear; it moves to its own route and a much stronger join page.

Headline figures are from claglobal.com; the country count is taken from its network and alliance lists. Case studies are sample content.
