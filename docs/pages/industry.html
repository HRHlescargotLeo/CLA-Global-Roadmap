Prototype 2 of 5 · Industry page

What we're proposing

A page per industry, built from one template: the cross-border issues businesses in that sector raise most, the member firms that list it as a specialism, and case studies and insights tagged with it.

Why

There are no industry pages on claglobal.com, and industries don't appear in the navigation. Yet the firm profiles already list them: seven for CLA Malta, nine for S&W. Most of what an industry page needs is sitting in the profiles, so the effort is mainly in writing the issues section and tagging insights properly.

Shown with manufacturing and distribution. Use the menu under the title to switch industry; other industries show placeholder issues.
